Why not just divide?

A single length ÷ stock is easy — Kaatbachat does exactly that and skips the fancy framing. But combining different lengths across rods to minimise offcut has no closed form; over- or under-buying stock is real money.

This runs first-fit-decreasing: place the longest pieces first, into the first rod that still has room.
